🗺️
Porur is most saturated
413 restaurants — 14% more than Velachery (362). Intense competition zone with below-avg rating 3.47.
☁️
Cloud Kitchens lead on quality
Avg rating 3.68 — highest of all segments. Delivery-first model focuses on food quality over ambiance.
⭐
Only 1% are "Excellent"
Just 119 restaurants score 4.5+. The top tier is extremely rare — a strong differentiator for premium brands.
🍜
Chinese is #1 cuisine
3,527 restaurants serve Chinese — more than South Indian (3,079). Chennai's taste is diverse and pan-Indian.
🛵
Delivery dominates features
Home Delivery (8,477) and Indoor Seating (8,383) are near-universal. 1,018 are delivery-only cloud kitchens.
🏆
Nungambakkam = quality hub
Avg rating 3.69 in 245 restaurants. Premium residential & commercial zone attracts better-quality outlets.
📉
Hotels score lowest
Hotel segment avg rating 3.45 — last among all segments. Hotel restaurants often rely on captive guests.
🌅
Breakfast is a growth niche
Only 2,556 offer breakfast — just 21.6% of restaurants. High demand potential near IT corridors.
📊
54% are "Good" rated
6,441 restaurants score 3.5–3.9. The bell curve is right-skewed — most outlets cluster just above average.
